Community & career development

Women in Motorsports North America

WIMNA connects people across motorsports through networking, educational events, mentorship, and professional development. Women with Drive is its annual summit for learning, meeting people in the industry, and exploring careers throughout racing.

Useful forStudents and early-career professionals interested in technical, business, and operational roles.

Collegiate Racing Series

CRS creates opportunities for college students to take part in wheel-to-wheel racing and sim racing. A collegiate program can involve much more than driving: race preparation, team operations, data, sponsorship, and communications all matter.

Useful forCollege students interested in running and supporting a race team.

Purdue Collegiate EV Grand Prix

College teams build, develop, and race electric karts while combining engineering with driving, data analysis, strategy, and team operations. The collegiate competition also includes design and energy-efficiency work.

Useful forCollege students across technical and team-support roles.

High School EV Grand Prix

High-school teams build and race electric karts through a school-supported program. Students develop practical STEM and teamwork skills alongside a STEM report, outreach work, and test-and-tune events.

Useful forHigh-school students, teachers, and school teams.

Formula SAE — Combustion

University teams design, manufacture, test, and compete with a small formula-style race car. Students practice mechanical design, vehicle dynamics, testing, cost analysis, and project management while learning how their decisions work on a real vehicle.

Useful forStudents who want hands-on design, manufacturing, testing, or team-management experience.

Formula SAE ↗

Baja SAE

Students design and build an off-road vehicle, then test it through competition. The work emphasizes durability, suspension, fabrication, manufacturing, troubleshooting, and teamwork in demanding conditions.

Useful forStudents interested in off-road vehicles, mechanical design, fabrication, and testing.

Baja SAE ↗

Formula SAE Electric

Electric Formula SAE teams integrate the mechanical vehicle with an electric powertrain, controls, and high-voltage systems. Vehicle dynamics, packaging, data, reliability, manufacturing, and safe testing remain central to the project.

Useful forStudents interested in electric vehicles and the full range of technical and operational team roles.

Formula SAE Electric information ↗

Collegiate Solar Racing

Solar racing teams develop efficient vehicles for events such as the American Solar Challenge and Formula Sun Grand Prix. Students work across aerodynamics, structures, electrical systems, energy management, data analysis, and race operations.

Useful forStudents interested in vehicle development, efficiency, systems engineering, and endurance competition.

American Solar Challenge ↗

eNASCAR College iRacing Series

A collegiate sim-racing series that lets eligible students represent their schools in organized competition. It is a way to practice race preparation, driving, telemetry review, and communication without operating a physical race car.

Useful forCollege students interested in competitive simulation and driver-performance analysis.

NABME

The National Advisory Board for Motorsports Education was jointly established by IMSA and Collegiate Racing Series to strengthen connections between higher education and the motorsports industry. It is an education and industry initiative, not a racing championship.

Useful forStudents and educators following motorsports education pathways.

Newly established — August 2026NABME was founded in August 2026. More details on its initiatives and plans are expected as the board develops its programs; follow the official IMSA and CRS announcements for updates.

Collegiate iRacing League

CIL organizes collegiate sim-racing competition across multiple series. Students can practice driving, telemetry analysis, race preparation, and team communication. It is separate from the eNASCAR College iRacing Series.

Useful forCollege students who want to compete or build analysis skills using simulation.

National Association of Intercollegiate Racing

NAIR connects college students with motorsports education and organized intercollegiate sim racing. Its program brings technical learning together with business, marketing, and leadership experience.

Useful forCollege and university students interested in motorsports education and team participation.

Sports Car Club of America — SCCA

SCCA offers ways to take part in autocross, track events, time trials, road racing, and event operations. Regional clubs are a practical starting point for meeting local racers, learning how events run, and finding a discipline that fits your interests.

Useful forStudents and enthusiasts interested in grassroots competition, driving, and volunteering.

National Auto Sport Association — NASA

NASA organizes High Performance Driving Events (HPDE), time trials, and road racing through regional programs. HPDE provides structured driving instruction, while time trials and racing offer further routes into competition.

Useful forDrivers and students interested in track experience, car preparation, and regional racing.
