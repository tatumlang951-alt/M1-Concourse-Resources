Learn by doing

Projects you can build

Start small and finish something you can explain. Most of these projects are beginner-friendly; two stretch projects add hands-on electronics, design, and validation when you are ready for more.

PROJECT 01

Tire degradation tool

Use a run of lap times to estimate pace falloff. Separate out-laps, traffic, cautions, and changing fuel load before fitting a simple trend.

What to makeA plot, a short explanation of your exclusions, and a comparison of two runs.
SkillsExcel · Python · Data analysis
Beginner

PROJECT 02

Driver pace comparison

Compare lap time, sectors, braking points, throttle application, and minimum speeds for two drivers or two laps. Align data by distance and check track conditions.

What to makeAn annotated lap overlay and three specific areas to review.
SkillsTelemetry · Excel · MATLAB / Python
Beginner

PROJECT 03

Kart balance notebook

Record setup, conditions, driver comments, and lap-time response. Describe understeer or oversteer by corner and phase rather than using only “tight” or “loose.”

What to makeA run sheet and a short conclusion about one controlled setup change.
SkillsVehicle dynamics · Testing · Documentation
Beginner

PROJECT 04

Multi-sensor data logger & CAN bench

Build a bench-top logger that synchronizes two or three sensors and reads messages from a CAN simulator or spare test device. Decode signals, calibrate measurements, detect missing samples, and compare the recorded signals with reference values. Start on a bench before considering vehicle installation.

What to makeA working logger, wiring diagram, signal definitions, and a short validation report.
SkillsEmbedded programming · CAN · Instrumentation · Validation
Intermediate — stretch project

PROJECT 05

Tire temperature study

Record temperatures across the tread after comparable runs. Keep the measurement order and timing consistent, and note that surface temperature changes quickly.

What to makeA measurement sheet and a plot comparing runs.
SkillsTesting · Data collection · Excel
Beginner

PROJECT 06

Design, build & test a sensor bracket

Design a small kart sensor or data-logger bracket in CAD. Define its loads and clearances, choose a material and manufacturing process, and compare a simple hand calculation or FEA prediction with measured deflection on a bench prototype. Revise the design using the test result.

What to makeCAD and drawings, a prototype, a basic load test, and a comparison of predicted versus measured behavior.
SkillsCAD · Manufacturing · FEA · Physical testing
Intermediate — stretch project

PROJECT 07

Track map generator

Plot GPS or simulation coordinates, then color the path by speed or another channel. Check coordinate units and remove obvious bad points.

What to makeA readable track map with a legend and labeled units.
SkillsPython · GPS · Visualization
Beginner

PROJECT 08

Setup & inventory workbook

Create a simple setup log with conditions, driver feedback, and tire history. Add an inventory sheet so a team can track spare parts and consumables.

What to makeA reusable workbook with clear inputs and a completed example.
SkillsExcel · Operations · Documentation
Beginner

PROJECT 09

Race strategy calculator

Estimate fuel and pit windows using a small set of inputs. Compare a few scenarios and show how the answer changes with fuel use, pit loss, or tire falloff.

What to makeA transparent spreadsheet or script with stated assumptions.
SkillsExcel · Python · Decision-making
Intermediate

PROJECT 10

Sensor calibration project

Choose a temperature, position, or pressure sensor and compare it with a known reference across a useful range. Record repeatability as well as the best-fit calibration.

What to makeA calibration curve, units, and an error estimate.
SkillsInstrumentation · DAQ · Testing
Beginner

PROJECT 11

Simple aero experiment

Use a model and tuft testing, or a basic CFD study, to investigate one clear airflow question. Change one feature at a time and explain the limits of your setup.

What to makeBefore-and-after observations with a controlled procedure.
SkillsAerodynamics · Design of experiments · Testing
Beginner–intermediate

PROJECT 12

Race-weekend report & sponsor update

Turn public or simulated race data into a short report. Include results, a useful pace comparison, a simple budget or logistics lesson, and a concise sponsor-facing summary.

What to makeAn engineering summary plus a one-page communications update.
SkillsAnalysis · Writing · Marketing · Budgeting
Beginner
