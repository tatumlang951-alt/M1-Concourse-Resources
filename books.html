The reading list

Books worth spending time with

This reading list was compiled primarily from recommendations from race engineers and technical directors I’ve worked with or interviewed, along with books from my own research and reading. If you’re only choosing a few, start with the first four.

The first four are outlined as recommended starting points. After book 5, the order does not indicate importance.

01
Start here

Race Car Vehicle Dynamics

William F. Milliken & Douglas L. Milliken

Vehicle Dynamics

A foundational race engineering reference for understanding vehicle dynamics and car setup.

Book information ↗
02
Start here

Tire and Vehicle Dynamics

Hans B. Pacejka

Tires

A foundational tire-modeling reference, including Magic Formula concepts. Tire modeling is valuable for students pursuing race engineering.

03
Start here

Analysis Techniques for Racecar Data Acquisition

Jorge Segers

Data Analysis

Recommended to me by Aylor Huneycutt. A strong introduction and guide to motorsports data analysis.

04
Start here

Suspension Geometry and Computation

John C. Dixon

Suspension

My favorite of the suspension books listed.

05

The Science of Vehicle Dynamics: Handling, Braking, and Ride of Road and Race Cars

Massimo Guiggiani

Vehicle Dynamics

A rigorous treatment of vehicle dynamics, covering handling, braking, and ride for road and race cars.

Publisher information ↗
06

Chassis Design: Principles and Analysis

Maurice Olley; edited by William F. Milliken & Douglas L. Milliken

Car Design

Chassis design fundamentals and the reasoning behind vehicle layout and suspension choices.

Milliken book catalog ↗
07

Tune to Win

Carroll Smith

Car Setup

An easier read than Race Car Vehicle Dynamics; approachable as a practical setup guide.

08

The Unfair Advantage

Mark Donohue with Paul Van Valkenburgh

Racing / Car Development

Donohue's account of racing and developing cars.

09

Vehicle Handling Dynamics

Masato Abe

Vehicle Dynamics

An engineering treatment of vehicle handling and its mathematical models.

10

Generalized Vehicle Dynamics

Daniel E. Williams

Vehicle Dynamics

A vehicle-modeling reference written by a Purdue professor.

SAE book information ↗
11

Racing Car Design and Development

Len Terry & Alan Baker

Car Design

Racing-car design choices and the process of developing a car.

Preview / book information ↗
12

Competition Car Suspension

Allan Staniforth

Suspension

Suspension layout, geometry, and setup for competition cars.

13

The Perfect Car

Nick Skeens

Biography

John Barnard's career and approach to racing-car design. I have not read it yet.

Publisher information ↗
14

Basic Vehicle Dynamics and Suspension Design

R. Dale White

Vehicle Dynamics

Vehicle-dynamics fundamentals applied to suspension design.

Book information ↗
15

Inspired to Design

Nigel Bennett

Biography / Car Design

A racing-car designer's career and the development of his cars.

16

Vibration Control of Vehicle Suspension Systems

Haiping Du, Panshuo Li & Donghong Ning

Vibrations

Suspension vibration and control methods. I have not read it yet.

Publisher information ↗
17

The Dynamics of the Race Car

Danny Nowlan

Vehicle Dynamics

Race-car behavior, modeling and simulation.

18

Race Car Design

Derek Seward

Car Design

A practical engineering approach to designing a race car.

19

Beast

Jade Gurss

Motorsport History / Engineering Case Study

The story of the engine project behind Penske's 1994 Indianapolis 500 effort.

20

How to Build a Car

Adrian Newey

Biography / Car Design

Newey's career and approach to racing-car design. I have not read it yet.

21

Total Competition: Lessons in Strategy from Formula One

Ross Brawn & Adam Parr

Strategy

Racing leadership, competition and decision-making. I have not read it yet.

22

Formula One Race Engineering: Optimizing a Driver's Performance with a Winning Method

Ernesto Desiderio

General Race Engineering

A method for analyzing and improving driver performance. I have not read it yet.

Preview / book information ↗
23

A Practical Guide to Race Car Data Analysis

Bob Knox

Data Analysis

Practical methods for turning race-car data into useful conclusions.

24

Making Sense of Squiggly Lines

Chris Brown

Data Analysis

Focuses on driver performance, particularly when working with less experienced or nonprofessional drivers.

25

Learn How To Master The Art Of Kart Driving

Terence Dove

Kart Driving

I have not read it, but it was recommended to me for drivers moving from car racing to karting.

Preview / book information ↗
26

Two-Stroke Tuner's Handbook

Gordon Jennings

Two-Stroke Engines

An older reference useful for understanding engines from the Yamaha KT100 era. Newer references may also be worth exploring.

27

An Introduction to Statistical Learning

Gareth James, Daniela Witten, Trevor Hastie & Robert Tibshirani (R edition)

Statistics

Statistical-learning methods useful for modeling and data analysis.
